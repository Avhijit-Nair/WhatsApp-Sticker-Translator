Sticker Translator

A Mac app that sits quietly alongside WhatsApp Desktop and automatically labels Spanish stickers with an English translation and what they really mean, entirely on-device.

Scope → PRD

Scope said: "a small label shows up next to it."

Now we know exactly what that means: a dark tooltip that says Translating…, then shows literal + meaning, follows the sticker as you scroll, hides off-screen, closes with ✕ and stays closed, and shows error + retry on failure. Stickers with no text or English text get nothing.

Acceptance criteria

Control panel and toggle
  • Turning on "Always translate images" starts automatic translation
  • Turning it off stops translation and removes tooltips (assumption)
  • Panel hides to the menu bar and reopens from there
  • Language shows Spanish → English
Automatic detection
  • Visible Spanish stickers and images get a tooltip without the user doing anything
  • Stickers with no text or English text get no tooltip
Translation tooltip
  • Shows "Translating… please wait a moment" while working
  • Then shows the literal translation + a short meaning explanation
  • Sits beside the sticker, never covering it
  • ✕ closes it; it stays closed after scrolling away and back
  • On failure: error message + retry, and retry tries again
Scroll tracking
  • Tooltips move with their sticker while scrolling
  • They hide when the sticker leaves the visible area and reappear when it returns
Privacy
  • Translation works with the network turned off

Boundary

Deferred from the POC
  • Hindi and other languages: on-device Hindi script reading is riskier
  • Remembering closed tooltips across restarts
  • Android / Pixel overlay
Non-goals
  • iPhone (no overlays allowed) · cloud AI (privacy) · redrawing stickers · plain-text translation
Open questions (none block the spec)
  • Show both lines when literal = meaning? (build)
  • Does the toggle stay on after a restart? Assumption: starts off. (build)
  • Is on-device explanation quality good enough? (4-spec, check early)
