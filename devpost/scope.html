WhatsApp Image Translator (working title)

An always-on Mac overlay that spots Spanish text in images and stickers in WhatsApp Desktop and labels each one with a literal translation and what it actually means, entirely on-device.

The unique kernel

Zero steps. You flip one toggle, and every Spanish sticker gets a label with the literal translation and the meaning: the slang, the tone, the joke. Circle to Search and Lens only work when you stop and ask each time.

The core loop: what the demo shows

Illustrative mockup only. The real layout and wording get decided in the PRD.

Who it's for

You, and anyone chatting on WhatsApp with someone whose stickers and memes are in another language. Today they guess, ask, or leave the chat to use Lens.

Why it matters

some of the stickers are in Spanish and I don't have a tool that instantly translates

Goal: stand out to non-expert judges. Everyone understands this in two seconds.

What "working" looks like

WhatsApp Desktop is open and the toggle is on. A funny exchange plays out. She sends a Spanish sticker, and a label appears beside it with the translation and the slang explained, without you touching anything.

The "oh, that's cool" moment: the label appears on its own and explains the joke, not just the words. Nothing leaves the Mac

Boundary

Now: proof of concept6
  • macOS only, WhatsApp Desktop only
  • On/off toggle: "always translate images"
  • Automatically finds Spanish text in visible images and stickers
  • Labels next to each image on a transparent always-on-top layer: literal + meaning
  • Spanish → English
  • Fully on-device: screenshots and chat content never leave the Mac
Later4
  • Android overlay on your Pixel
  • Other messaging apps and language pairs
  • Better slang explanations, possibly a model customized for this (your model-compression interest)
  • Translating text messages too
Explicitly cut4
  • iPhone: iOS doesn't allow overlays on other apps
  • Cloud AI APIs: would send private chat screenshots off the device
  • Redrawing the image: labels keep the original visible and are simpler
  • Text-message translation: phones already do this
Earlier directions set aside3
  • Graphene coatings evidence explorer: customers unlikely to use it; poor fit for non-expert judges
  • Automated model compression: hard to show visually, too large for 2–4 hours
  • Agent-friendly websites: secondhand idea

Notes for later phases

  • The demo video is public and shows a real chat. Stage an exchange your girlfriend is comfortable sharing.
  • On-device explanation quality is the main risk. The spec checks which local models fit on your MacBook Air M4.
